## 4. Gold Scoring Formula

**Composite Resilience Score** (0–100, higher = more resilient):

```
overall_score = 0.40 × path_diversity_score
              + 0.30 × hazard_exposure_score
              + 0.30 × telemetry_health_score
```

| Component | Weight | Source Data | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| path_diversity_score | 40% | service_path + shared_risk_group | SRLG diversity between working and protect paths. 100 = fully diverse, 0 = shared fate |
| hazard_exposure_score | 30% | geographic_hazard_event × span geom_wkt | Inverse of active hazard overlap. 100 = no hazards nearby, 0 = multiple active hazards |
| telemetry_health_score | 30% | resource_performance_measure + resource_alarm | Optical health: OSNR, BER, power. 100 = nominal, degraded by alarms and performance anomalies |
